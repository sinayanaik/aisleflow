AisleFlow: is it better?

Yes on the maps it was designed for, no on open ones, and by a wide margin against both published baselines everywhere. This page lets you check that claim on any metric, on any map, with the interval and the p-value attached.

Read this first — two things that make the raw numbers misleading

Every run here is saturated. Tasks arrive faster than any planner can serve them, so the backlog grows all run and throughput is not "how busy it was" — it is the warehouse's service capacity under that planner. That is why 0.50 against 0.31 tasks per timestep is a 61% difference and not a rounding error.

Service time only counts tasks that finished. A planner that gives up on the hard tasks reports a beautiful mean service time. Both external baselines do exactly that here: near-zero throughput beside some of the best service times in the dataset, because the only tasks they finish are the easy early ones. Never rank two planners by service time unless their throughput is comparable — switch the metric selector below to see it.
